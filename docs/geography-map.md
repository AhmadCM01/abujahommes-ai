# Abuja Federal Capital Territory (FCT) Geography & Council Ownership Map

## Overview & Statutory Integrity
The Federal Capital Territory (FCT) is divided into **exactly six (6) Area Councils**:
1. **AMAC** (Abuja Municipal Area Council) — Headquarters: Garki
2. **Bwari** — Headquarters: Bwari Central
3. **Gwagwalada** — Headquarters: Gwagwalada Central
4. **Kuje** — Headquarters: Kuje Town
5. **Kwali** — Headquarters: Kwali Central
6. **Abaji** — Headquarters: Abaji Central

Every residential district, commercial layout, satellite town, and planned estate in the AbujaHommes AI intelligence platform belongs to **EXACTLY ONE** Area Council. Cross-council dual ownership is strictly invalid, and all property searches, filters, AI valuations, and listing submissions enforce this single source of truth.

| **Lugbe & Airport Road Estates** | **AMAC** | Often mistakenly grouped into Kuje or Gwagwalada due to Airport Road proximity. | **Maintained in AMAC**: Lugbe is an integral part of AMAC (Kabusa/Airport Road electoral wards). | **Dawaki** | **Bwari** | Nestled under Katampe hills, sometimes confused with AMAC's Katampe Extension. | **Enforced in Bwari**: Dawaki belongs exclusively to Bwari. |
| **Dutse / Dutse Alhaji** | **Bwari** | Frequently confused with AMAC. | **Enforced in Bwari**: All Dutse sectors (Alhaji, Makaranta, Sagwari, Pege) belong strictly to Bwari. |
| **Mpape** | **Bwari** | Overlooks Maitama across the rocky ridge, leading some brokers to label it "Maitama Extension / AMAC". | **Enforced in Bwari**: Mpape is strictly a Bwari Area Council district. |
| **Ushafa** | **Bwari** | Scenic pottery community. | **Enforced in Bwari**: Ushafa ward belongs to Bwari. |
| **Karu & Nyanya** | **AMAC** | Straddles FCT / Nasarawa State border; often confused with Karu Local Government Area in Nasarawa. | **Enforced in AMAC**: FCT Nyanya and FCT Karu are AMAC satellite towns. (Out-of-FCT Nasarawa towns are excluded). |
